# Log 035 — Gate 2: the guard checks the way to a target, not only its end (zones and the other arm) (2026-10-27, ~12:45)

## Why
The goldfish review of goal.md (2026-10-27) found that the guard only checked a target, not the way to it.
- `Guard.limit` returned the target unchanged as soon as the target itself was allowed. Only a target inside a zone made it search the straight joint line.
- So a move between two allowed poses that **passes through** a zone (the table box) went through.
- log-034's arm-to-arm cut had the same gap: when both arms move at once, the poses in between were not checked.
- goal Gate 2: "check every executed setpoint".

## What changed (tests first)
**`driver/openarm_guard.py`**
- New `SWEEP_STEP_DEG = 1.0` and `sweep_steps(prev, q)`: the straight joint line from the last pose to the target is checked at least every 1 deg of the joint that moves most (about 1 cm of hand at full reach).
- `Guard.limit`:
  - It finds the **first step** that breaks a zone and cuts back to the last allowed point inside that step (16 halvings, as before).
  - The reason says "(on the way to the target)" when the target itself is allowed.
  - **Unchanged:** an arm already a little outside (it lags its target at an edge) may still move back inside, and only the end is cut. Otherwise it would be stuck outside. Well outside still raises.

**`driver/openarm_pair.py`, `cut`**: the same walk against the other arm. The other arm's capsules are now computed once per check (it does not move during one check).

**`driver/openarm_v2.py`, `write()`, found while doing this**
- The new cut made an existing test fail: `test_a_smooth_arm_may_be_commanded_ahead_of_where_it_lags`.
- That test makes sure a 45 deg jump in one `write()` is refused (`MAX_STEP_DEG` = 20; "a wrong angle mapping looks like this").
- The arm-to-arm walk had cut the target short **before** that check, so the shortened step passed and **the wrong-mapping warning was hidden**.
- Fixed: the jump check now runs on **Dream's requested target, before any safety cut** (new `_refuse_a_jump`, also used for the per-joint check after the cut). This applies to the zone cut as well.
