## 1. False "gripper holds an object": a slow gripper, not an object
- The user confirmed: **no bottle or object was in the gripper.** All six "holds an object" lines of the day came while the gripper travelled far: closing for the park (62 to 64 deg behind), opening for Dream's Home (6 to 9 deg), closing in a run (5 deg).
- **Cause:** the gripper runs on a low gain (KP 12, lowered after the 2026-09-21 over-temperature), with its target leading at most 8 deg, so it travels slowly (a full 64 deg close takes more than 2 s). The log-032 rule looked only at the lead (more than 4 deg for 2 s), not at whether the gripper still moved. The false hold then cut the lead to 4 deg, which made the gripper slower still, and probably slowed the parks.
- **Fix (tests first):** a hold needs the lead over 4 deg **and** less than 1 deg of movement in those 2 s (`GRIPPER_STALL_MOVE_DEG`). A slow gripper on its way keeps resetting the count; one squeezing an object does not move and still gets the light grip.
  - Test `test_a_gripper_that_closes_slowly_is_not_taken_for_one_holding_an_object` (creeping closed at 5 deg/s, 4 s): red first, then green. The blocked-gripper test still passes.
