# Log 010 — Preparing to move the arms: Dream's OpenArm v2 model vs the fork's joint convention (2026-10-23)

## Question
Before switching the driver to `Arm`: does the driver's identity mapping (fork logical degrees -> radians in Dream's `openarm2_faas` model)
make Dream's idea of the hand pose match the real arm? Nothing was moved or switched on for this; all of it is offline kinematics.

## Method (scratchpad `mapping*.py`)
Forward kinematics of Dream's `dream_ik/assets/openarm2_faas_<side>.urdf` vs the v10 URDF generated in log-001. For each side, search for per-joint
sign and offset (offsets in 0 / +-90 / 180 deg for the wrist, signs +-1) and a fixed base and tool transform so that both chains give the same
hand pose for 40 random joint sets.

## Findings
- Same side matches same side exactly (error ~1e-12): Dream left = Enactic left chain, Dream right = Enactic right chain. Cross-side does not match.
  Link lengths and joint limits are equal on all joints (j1 -200..80, j2 -190..10, j3 +-90, j4 0..140, j5 +-90, j6 +-45, j7 +-90 for left).
- Joints 1-4: an exact solution exists with identity (sign +1, offset 0) (and an alternative with signs -1,-1,+1,-1). Identity agrees with the equal limits.
- Wrist (joints 5-7): EVERY exact solution has a 90 deg offset on joint 5 (and the joint 7 offset/sign is not determined by hand orientation alone,
  the wrist has equivalent flipped solutions). Identity on the wrist is NOT a solution. A 90 deg offset on joint 5 contradicts the equal +-90 limits
  (the physical range would not fit inside the model's range).
- Conclusion: the driver's identity mapping (MODEL_SIGN = 1, MODEL_ZERO_RAD = 0) is probably wrong for the wrist; with it, Dream would compute
  a wrong hand pose and send wrong joint targets. Moving under Dream control with it is not safe.
- Possibly related: the probe readings left j5 -88 deg, right j3 +89.5 deg, right j5 64 deg (log-006) look like 90 deg convention or zero differences;
  not proven.
