# Log 044 — The 20 deg check on a smooth arm: the first request against the measured arm; Dream's own jumps during a run warn up to 40 deg (2026-10-27, ~14:25)

## Why
In log-043, run 4 (14:16:55), Dream's **own** request for the right wrist (J5) jumped 22.5 deg from its previous one. Most likely its IK switched to another wrist solution near J5's limit. The 20 deg check, meant to catch a wrong angle mapping, refused it and ended the run.
At 14:17:00 Dream's Home was then refused: its first request (from the measured arm, J5 about 55 deg) was compared with the last **target** (78.5 deg), which the wrist never reached.
The user approved the proposal of log-043 §3.

## What changed (`driver/openarm_v2.py`, `write()`; tests first)
On a smooth arm (always on in the station):
- **The first request of a run, or after a pause** (no request in the last 0.5 s), is compared with **the measured arm**, no longer with a target the arm may not have reached. Over 20 deg (`MAX_STEP_DEG`) it is still refused, because that is what a wrong angle mapping looks like.
- **During a run** (fresh requests), a jump between two of Dream's own requests of **20 to 40 deg** (`MAX_RUN_JUMP_DEG`) is a **warning** (rate-limited): "Dream's request jumped … carried out at the tracker's speed". The tracker carries it out at its gentle limits, and the guard checks the way (zones, body, gripper, the other arm).
- Over 40 deg between two requests is still refused.

Without smoothing nothing changes: every target is sent as it is, so the 20 deg check stays as it was.

Tests (`driver/test_openarm_smooth.py`, +3; two red first):
1. A 22.5 deg J5 jump between requests during a run: a warning, no error.
2. A 45 deg jump between requests: still refused.
3. After a pause, a request next to the measured arm passes although the last target was 23 deg away; a request 30 deg from the measured arm is refused ("from the arm").

The existing "a 45 deg jump in one write is still refused" and "a real jump between two requests is still refused" (non-smooth) still pass. Driver suite **172 passed** (the known `test_blank_camera…` fails). `openarm_v2.py` `b3e03eccc36f`.
