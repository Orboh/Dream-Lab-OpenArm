**Discrepancies, official v10 vs Dream's `openarm2_faas` (`python stage2.py discrepancies --side left|right`, both arms give the same result):**

**Indirect evidence from the coupling of the joints in that block** (correlation of the angles): joints whose axes are parallel in a model move together when a hand pushes the arm.
| Pair | Parallel in | Correlation |
|---|---|---|
| J2 and J6 | v10 (both about x) | **-0.91** |
| J1 and J7 | v10 (both about y) | **+0.84** |
| J1 and J4 | both models (about y) | -0.71 |

**Strength: suggestive, not conclusive.** One hand-made motion, mixed shoulder and wrist motion, and a simple assumption (parallel joints co-move); the signs of the couplings were not derived.
