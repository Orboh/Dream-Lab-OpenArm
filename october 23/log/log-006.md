# Log 006 — Read-only probe of the follower motors (2026-10-23)

## Why
After `canup.sh` the user still saw a red light on the arms. Check, without switching anything on, whether every
follower motor answers on the CAN-FD links.

## What was run
- All four links first checked passively: `ip -d link show` -> up, `<FD>`, 1M/5M, ERROR-ACTIVE, 0 errors.
- Then a scratchpad script (`.../scratchpad/probe.py`) opened `ReadOnlyArm` (driver/openarm_v2.py) on `can_fol_l` and
  `can_fol_r` and sent one state query per motor (no enable, no MIT frame, nothing written to the arms).

## Result
- All 16 motors answered (ids 1-8 on each arm) through the FD path: the driver's FD framing works on the real bus.
- All 16 report status DISABLED, no fault codes. Temperatures 26-30 C.
- Logical angles (offset-corrected), degrees. Left: j1 -0.96, j2 -0.75, j3 -0.95, j4 0.39, j5 -88.20, j6 -8.02,
  j7 3.64, gripper 1.06. Right: j1 1.99, j2 -0.67, j3 89.50, j4 3.04, j5 64.27, j6 -4.07, j7 -1.49, gripper 0.21.
- Left j5 (-88), right j3 (+89.5) and right j5 (64) are far from the rest; either the arms are physically turned there,
  or the stored homing offsets are stale. Not decided; needs a look at the arms.

## Open
- Meaning of the red light: all motors disabled is consistent with it, but the Damiao LED colour codes were not
  checked. Not verified that enabling turns it green.
- The three large angles above.

## Addendum — switching the followers on (user said "switch it on")
- Scratchpad script `hold.py`: the driver's `Arm` on `can_fol_l` and `can_fol_r`, each motor enabled and sent its own
  measured position as target (kp/kd = the fork's gains), refreshed at 50 Hz for 60 s, then `torque_off()`.
- Both arms switched on; for 60 s every motor stayed enabled, no fault, drift at most 0.02 deg.
- Switch-off: left confirmed. Right reported "joints 1-5 did not confirm switching off". A follow-up read-only query showed
  all 16 motors disabled (status 0), so it was a false alarm: the reply to a disable frame carries the status from BEFORE the
  command (same for enable).
- Driver bug fixed: `Arm._disable` now confirms with a fresh state query after the disable frame. The test fake bus now
  replies with the pre-command status like the real motors. 17 tests pass.
- Not seen from here: the LED colour while on (the user's report is needed). The three odd angles from the probe were not
  explained; holding in place did not depend on them.

## Addendum — first move: left follower joint_7 nudge (user chose "small test nudge", left follower)
- Scratchpad `nudge.py`: `Arm` on `can_fol_l`, all motors held at their own start position (fork gains), joint_7 moved +5 deg
  with a smooth profile over 2 s, held 1 s, back over 2 s, held 1 s, then `torque_off()`. Aborts if a motor leaves the enabled
  state, another joint drifts > 3 deg, or joint_7 is > 4 deg from its target.
- Result: joint_7 3.67 -> 8.19 deg and back to 3.86 deg; worst tracking error 0.66 deg; no abort; left arm switched off and confirmed.
- Open: which way the wrist physically turned for +5 deg (needed to check the sign in MODEL_SIGN for Dream's model);
  the LED colour while on; the three odd angles from the probe.

## Addendum — "move the left arm 10 degree back": left follower joint_1 +10 deg
- Interpretation (mine, not confirmed by the user): "back" = joint_1, the shoulder swing. From the generated URDF the left joint_1 axis
  is the body's lateral axis, positive = hanging arm swings toward -x (backward if x is forward); limits -200..80 fit that
  (a long forward/overhead range, 80 deg back). Direction not yet checked by eye.
- Scratchpad `move_j1.py` (nudge.py with joint_1, +10 deg over 4 s, hold 20 s, back over 4 s): others held in place, aborts on
  enabled-state loss, drift > 3 deg, or > 4 deg tracking error. The arm was returned to start before switch-off because
  switching off leaves it limp and it would swing.
- Result: joint_1 -0.96 -> 8.13 deg (target 9.04: ~0.9 deg short at rest, load/friction at the fork's kp 240), held 20 s, back to -0.85 deg;
  worst tracking error 0.94 deg; no abort; left arm switched off and confirmed.
