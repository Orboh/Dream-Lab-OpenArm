# Log 026 — Gripper step limit, park time and the clearance of the raise and park path (2026-10-24 15:35 to 15:45)

## Goal
Fix the two problems the first live run showed (log-024): the gripper step that ended run 1, and the 62-second park that `dreamcli robot down` (it waits 60 s) cut short. Offline, with tests; one config apply at the end (station stopped, arms off).

## 1. Gripper step (`driver/openarm_v2.py`)
Before: a gripper target more than 20 deg from the last target raised `ArmError` ("a wrong angle mapping looks like this") and ended the run (run 1: -33.9 to -4.1 deg). The gripper angle is measured and Dream opens and closes it in big steps, so this was no sign of a wrong mapping.
Now: a gripper target further than `GRIPPER_MAX_STEP_DEG` (20 deg) from the last one is **approached in steps of 20 deg** (a note in the log at most every 5 s); the tracker (gripper 80 deg/s) sets the real speed. The check for the seven joints is unchanged (still refuses a jump over 20 deg).

## 2. Park time
Cause: the taught rest-to-home path wanders (it was moved by hand: 25 and 23 points); the driver's smooth move slows down at every corner. Measured with the driver's own planner: left 64.7 s, right 61.7 s.
- `poses.simplify_path` (moved to `driver/openarm_poses.py`; `teach.simplify_path` keeps its name and default) and `EditorState.park_tolerance_deg`: when the bundle is saved, the home path is simplified to that tolerance **only if the shorter path keeps at least as much clearance from the zones** as the recorded one (a path that was clear must stay clear; a shortcut may lose at most 0.5 cm where the recorded path was already short). `workspace.py design --danger-only` uses 8 deg by default (`--park-tolerance-deg`).
- Result with the real paths: **right 61.7 s to 28.2 s (13 points), left 64.7 s to 39.6 s (14 points)**; both under the 45 s limit.
- The driver logs a **warning** when any smooth move plans longer than 45 s (`LONG_MOVE_WARN_S`): it names `dreamcli robot down` and the 60 s.
- **Preflight** has a new check: the raise and park along the home path take less than 45 s for each **active** arm (a locked arm is not raised or parked, so it is not checked).

## 3. The raise and park path was never checked against the zones (found while doing 2)
The editor's clash check and the preflight "clear of every zone" looked only at the **built-in** rest-to-ready path; the driver follows the **taught** path stored in the zones in force, and nothing tested that one. Also, the grasp point may touch the table box by design (for Dream's grasps), which is wrong for the driver's own move.
Now: `zonebuild.taught_path_problems` and `taught_path_slack_cm` check the **driver's planned smooth move** (every 0.1 s) with a **strict rule**: the grasp point keeps 2 cm from a zone, every link sphere 6 cm, no touching. Used by: the saved-path shortening, a new Save-card item ("the path from rest to home keeps clear of the danger zones"), a warning in `workspace.py build`, and a new **preflight check** for each active arm.
Finding on the real paths (box top at -35.5 cm): the **right** path keeps +2.9 cm of spare clearance; the **left** path is short of the required clearance by **1.8 cm** (its grasp point comes to 1.7 cm from the box, 21.7 s into the move). The left arm is locked, so preflight does not stop anything today; **unlocking the left arm would make preflight fail** until its path is taught again farther from the table (or the table margin is lowered).
