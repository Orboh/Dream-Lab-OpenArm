## Findings
1. **The new station file, zones and mapping run end to end** on Dream: five Start/Stop/Home cycles with no error, no refusal and normal timing.
2. **Dream asks for both arms in every run**, also for a one-arm prompt (as on 10-23; a lock holds the other arm in a live run).
3. **Direction-following could not be judged.** From the hanging pose the hand targets stay within about 1 cm of the measured hand for "5 cm up" and "to the left" (runs 2, 3, 5), and for "forward" after the first row (run 4). Only "go near the block" (run 1) asks the right hand to move (back and up, up to 16 cm). The arms hang straight down, a pose far from the home pose the cameras and the model expect and one where the forearm-twist joints (left J5, right J3) can turn 30 to 100 deg without moving the hand: the large joint swings are mostly that, not motion.
4. **First row of a run:** in run 4 the first target after Start was 34 to 35 cm from the measured hand, the same offset as the end of the Home move: Dream starts from the home pose (the ramp, `ramp_s 3`, covers it on a live run from home). Not verified further.
5. **The Home move from a far pose crosses the table box.** The straight joint-space blend from hanging to the home sweeps the hand through the box. On a live run the driver's own raise (along the recorded path) puts the arm at the home first, so the Home move starts there; the case that matters is a Reset when an arm is far from the home (for example low, near the table): the guard then cuts at the box edge instead of killing the run.
6. `clamps` in the inference log (276 in run 1): the control loop limited targets that were far from the measured pose; expected when the arm cannot follow, to be re-read on a live run.
