## Why Dream-1 does not grasp (evidence from today's runs)
**What we saw:** in 10 grasp runs Dream-1 never closed on the block or the bottle.
- It swung the right shoulder up to 90 deg (block run 1).
- It reached into the robot's middle, the column and the other arm (bottle runs, both sessions).
- It went down to the table near, but not around, the object.

The safety layer did not cause this: in most runs nothing was held at all, and where it was, Dream was already heading for the body or the table, not the object.

Likely reasons, most likely first:
1. **Zero-shot on this robot.** The checkpoint has not been trained on our arm, cameras and table. Dream's own docs: `custom.embodiment` is "the embodiment your fine-tune was trained on"; `dream/fine_tuning.py`: train Dream-1 on an uploaded dataset, then run the result. The fine-tune docs also recommend a cosine schedule "for specialization (a fine-tune on a single task)".
2. **The model acts on images only.** goal.md: "Dream-1's base model on a non-YAM arm masks robot state from the model". Dream feeds it small images (320x176). The head camera is a low side view (log-029) and was moved today, so an object of a few cm is a few pixels, and the views differ from any training data. Dream's fine-tune service even has `training_data()` "for comparing camera views". **Camera calibration is not used by Dream** (no intrinsics or extrinsics in its camera or client code), so calibrating would not help; consistent, informative views do.
3. **Our arm model is approximate:** ±2 cm depending on the pose (logs 025, 031). This matters for small objects even when the model aims right.
4. **The right wrist (J5) works close to Dream's limit** (log-031), so the IK switches solutions (log-043 run 4). It can make motions erratic near home.
