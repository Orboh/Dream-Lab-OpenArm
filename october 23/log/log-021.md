## Addendum — decision: keep the danger box top at the real surface (25 cm); the option to lower it is built in (2026-10-24)
**Decision (user): keep 25 cm for now.** The danger box top stays at the real table surface (lowering = 0), the safest setting, for the first supervised runs.
**Option kept for later, off by default:** lower the danger box top by a few cm so Dream's hand can get lower for grasping.
Why it may be needed: Dream keeps every 4 cm safety ball of the arm 2 cm off a keep-out box, so a ball centre stays 6 cm from the box; with the top at the surface the gripper links may not get low enough to grasp a small object lying on the table.
Limit: lowering by d cm lets the balls come d cm lower. **Up to 2 cm** the balls just touch the real surface and do not enter it; **above 2 cm** a ball dips into the real table (the editor and the report warn); the hard maximum is 4 cm.
**When to use it:** only if a supervised run shows Dream's hand stopping above the object (the hand does not go lower while the plan asks for it, or the log shows moves cut at a zone edge near the table).
**How (three ways, all just a number in the software; no calibration, no change on the table or the robot):**
1. On the page: Danger area tab, Table folder, field "lower the danger box top by (cm)" (0 to 4, step 0.5). Then Save on the Save tab.
2. At start-up: `workspace.py design ... --table-lower-cm 2`.
3. Ask me; I check it with `python stage3.py report ... --lower-cm 2` first (it prints the clearances and the warning).
It becomes active for Dream only when the bundle is applied (Stage 4). The saved bundle records it as `table_danger_lowered_cm`; loading a bundle restores the real surface height.
