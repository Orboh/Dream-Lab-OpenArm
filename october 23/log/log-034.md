# Log 034 — Gate 2/5: arm-to-arm check during a task (each arm's guard keeps 2 cm from the other arm) (2026-10-27, ~12:38)

## Why
log-033 checks the two arms against each other only for the raise, the park and Dream's Home, in the preflight. During a task Dream moves both arms from its model's targets, and each arm's guard knew only its own arm and the table. Both reviews of goal.md rated "no arm-to-arm check" a blocker (goal Gate 2: "both complete gripper/link envelopes against zones and each other"; Gate 5: stop on "inter-arm clearance below a threshold").

## What was built (tests first)
**`driver/openarm_pair.py`** (extends log-033):
- **A shared record** of both arms in the station's process. Both arms are `Arm` objects in one Python process, so a module-level record with a lock is enough.
  - `publish(side, "measured" | "target", q)`: each arm records its **measured pose on every control tick** and its **latest target after the guard**.
  - `others(side)`: the other arm's poses heard within **`FRESH_S` = 0.5 s**. **If nothing fresh was heard**, the other arm counts as hanging at its **rest pose** (`openarm_poses.REST_DEG`).
  - `reset()`: forgets all published poses.
- **`cut(side, prev, q, other_poses)`**: if the new target `q` keeps `MIN_GAP_M` (2 cm) from every pose of the other arm, it is returned unchanged. Otherwise the target is cut back along the straight joint line from `prev` to the last point that keeps 2 cm (12 halvings). This is the same "stop at the edge" behaviour as the guard's zone cut. If the arm is **already** closer than 2 cm, only a target that opens the gap is allowed; otherwise it stays at `prev`.

**`driver/openarm_v2.py`, `Arm._limited`** (the path of every Dream target through `write()`):
- After the zone guard, the target is cut by `pair.cut` against the other arm's measured pose and target.
- One warning per stretch, e.g. "arm left: stopped where it keeps 2 cm from the other arm (left hand -1.3 cm from the other arm's upper_arm); the target stays out of reach while the other arm is there".
- The kept target is published for the other arm.
- `_read_back` publishes the measured pose on every tick.

**`driver/conftest.py`**: the record is reset before and after every test, so no pose carries over between tests.

Speed: about **1 ms per target** (both arms' capsules, two poses of the other arm). The control tick is 10 ms.

## Tests (`driver/test_openarm_pair.py`, +4; red first, then green)

## Limits (open)
1. **Each new target is checked against the other arm's target and measured pose, not the way there.** When both arms move at once, the poses in between are not checked. This is the same open point as the zone guard (goal Gate 2, "every executed setpoint"): the guard checks where a move ends, not the way there.
2. **The hand capsule radius (6 cm) is an estimate**, as in log-033. The robot's body and head are not modelled.
3. **The rest-pose fallback assumes a silent arm hangs at rest.** An arm that is switched off but was left somewhere else (hand-guided) is not where the check thinks it is.
4. **Only within one process.** The station runs both arms in one process. The website's Go to home also runs both of its arms in its own process. Two separate programs driving one arm each would not see each other. The preflight's "nothing else holds the arms" check is meant to prevent that.
5. **`DryRunArm` does not apply or report this check yet.** The Gate 5 dry run should report arm-to-arm cuts too.
6. **Not run on the real arms.** The station picks it up at its next start.
