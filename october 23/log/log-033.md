# Log 033 — Gate 2/4: arm-to-arm clearance check (preflight replays raise, park and Dream's Home of both arms) (2026-10-27, ~12:30)

## Why
Both reviews of goal.md (elephant and goldfish, 2026-10-27) rated this a blocker.
- Nothing in the code checked one arm against the other. The guard, the preflight and the zones check each arm against the table only.
- goal.md asks for it:
  - Gate 2: "both complete gripper/link envelopes against zones and each other";
  - Gate 4: "paired swept-volume clearance … first arm held at Home while the second raises";
  - Gate 5: "predicted inter-arm clearances".
- Dream's Home moves every arm in a straight joint line to its home, all at once (`robots/home.py`, its docstring). The station raises the arms one after the other, as does the website's Go to home.

## What was built (tests first)
**`driver/openarm_pair.py`** (new): each arm is three **capsules** (a segment with a radius) in the robot's body frame.
- The capsules:
  - upper arm: joint 2 to joint 4, radius 4 cm (Dream's IK link radius);
  - forearm: joint 4 to joint 6, radius 4 cm;
  - hand: joint 6 to the grasp point, radius **6 cm**. This is an *estimate* of the open gripper (fingers 4.4 cm open each, plus the finger and housing), not measured.
- The arm bases sit at (0, ±3.1, 69.8) cm in the body frame (log-013), so the shoulders (joint 2) are 30.8 cm apart.
- The joint chain is the guard's (`ArmKinematics`, logical angles through the joint mapping in use). `ArmKinematics.chain()` was added to `openarm_zones.py` and returns the joint origins and the tcp.
- `gap_m(q_left, q_right)` gives the smallest distance between the two arms' surfaces (negative = overlap) and which parts. `problem()` flags a gap under **`MIN_GAP_M` = 2 cm** (the clearance Dream keeps from a zone).

**`workspace/zonebuild.arm_pair_problems()`** replays, every 0.1 s, what the arms really do together:
1. each arm's raise and park along its taught path (the driver's smooth move, `planned_path`) **while the other arm is at rest**;
2. the same **while the other arm holds its home** (the order of raising and parking is not fixed, so both cases are checked);
3. **Dream's Home**: both arms from rest to home at once in a straight joint line, at 50 points.

**Preflight**: new check "The two arms keep 2 cm apart: each raise and park (the other at rest or holding home) and Dream's Home of both at once". The preflight still takes about 1.3 s.
