## The shutdown incident
At 15:10:15 the user's stop made the station park the right arm along its recorded path before switching off: **61.7 s**. My `dreamcli robot down` waits 60 s ("up to 60 s"), then terminated and killed the station at about 15:11:20, so the station never logged "arms switched off". The recorder's last frames (15:11:20) show the right arm back at the hanging rest pose with **all eight motors still enabled**.
Action (with the user's yes): I opened the right arm read-only and sent the driver's own disable command (`Arm._disable`) to every motor: before, all eight enabled; after, all eight confirmed **disabled**. The arm was hanging at rest, so it only stayed hanging. The left arm's motors were already disabled. Station processes: none. `arms.lock` still names the dead process (stale).
Lessons: a recorded home path that takes longer than 60 s to park cannot be stopped cleanly with `dreamcli robot down`; the arm can be left energized. Proposals (not done): shorten the park (faster speed or a shorter path, under about 45 s); an energy-safe default in the motors (a communication timeout, `scripts/damiao_set_timeout.py` in openarm-orboh); never kill the station while it parks; check with a read-only query that the motors are off after every stop.
