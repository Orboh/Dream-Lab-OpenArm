# Log 007 — Wizard "Find arms" shows left and right as "Not found yet" (2026-10-23)

## Symptom
Step 3 (Connect arms) lists can_fol_l, can_fol_r, can_lead_l, can_lead_r; "Find arms" says Left arm and Right arm
"Not found yet" and asks to move any joint by hand.

## How the step works (from `robots/setup_session.py`, `setup_checks.py` in `~/dream-env`)
- The wizard opens every candidate device with the driver, disengages it (arm limp), reads it continuously, and calls a device
  "moved" when one of its joints changes by more than a threshold (about 5 deg) since the baseline. If two devices move at once
  it refuses ("several").
- `dreamcli setup` runs from `~/dream-env` (Python 3.12.12, python-can 4.5.0, dreamlabs 1.0.11), not from the project's `.venv`.

## What was observed (passive checks only; nothing sent to the arms)
- `ip -s link` packet counters, two samples 3 s apart: `can_fol_r` +464 tx / +464 rx (about 150 frames/s: the wizard is polling the
  right follower); `can_fol_l` unchanged at 68929 (no traffic at all from the wizard); `can_lead_l` 0 ever; `can_lead_r` unchanged.
- So the wizard is reading the RIGHT follower only. A left-arm movement cannot be seen because the left device is not being read.
- `~/.dreamlabs/arms/openarm-bimanual.json` exists from the earlier "From my URDF" route (base world, flange openarm_left_hand,
  tcp [0,0,0.05]) — written by the wizard earlier today, not by me.
- The wizard's `/api/status` needs the private URL printed by `dreamcli setup`; it was not used or bypassed.

## Open
- Why the left device is not opened (not ticked, or an open error shown in the page). Needs the page's per-adapter text.

## Cause found
- `can_fol_l` and `can_fol_r` are two channels of ONE PEAK USB adapter (both `parentdev 1-1:1.0`, no serial); likewise the
  leaders on `1-7:1.0`.
- `robots/setup_devices.py` (scan) names a serial-less CAN adapter by its USB port: id `can-port-<usb>`. Both follower links give
  the same id `can-port-1-1:1.0`, so the page ends up with ONE device and opens only one link (the right one, as the traffic showed).
  Moving the right arm was then "found" as whichever side the page asked for; the left device never exists.
- Way around: "typed" arms get their own ids (`typed-<address>`, opened as written), so typing `can_fol_l` and `can_fol_r` as arm
  addresses gives two devices. The stable names come from `scripts/canup.sh` (openarm-orboh), which must be re-run after a reboot
  or replug.
- Not verified yet: that the typed route works in the page; that saving writes `can_channel: can_fol_l/_r`.
