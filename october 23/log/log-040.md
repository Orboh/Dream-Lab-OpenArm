1. **`torque_off()` parks from any pose** (Dream's contract):
   - far from the path (over 20 deg), the arm goes back to the nearest point of its own path along a straight joint line checked every 1 deg by the guard (zones, body, gripper) and the arm-to-arm check (`Guard.way_problem`, `pair.way_problem`; a line starting slightly inside a zone counts if it leaves it and stays out);
   - then it parks along the path as before;
   - with no clear way back it **lets go slowly**: the stiffness goes to zero over 2 s, the damping stays (`_release_slowly`), so the arm settles instead of dropping.
