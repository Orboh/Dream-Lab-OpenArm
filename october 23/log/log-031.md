## IMPORTANT — joint 5: our range vs Dream's range, and the converter (11:35 to 11:45)

**Read this before teaching any home or pose.** Under the `official` joint mapping (log-019, the one in use, `~/.dreamlabs/model_mapping`), Dream's model counts joint 5 (forearm roll) from a different zero than the real arm:

| Arm | Dream's J5 = | Dream's J5 range | **Our J5 must be** |
|---|---|---|---|
| left | our J5 **+ 90 deg** | -90..+90 | **-180..0 deg** |
| right | our J5 **- 90 deg** | -90..+90 | **0..+180 deg** |

- **Only half of the real J5 range exists for Dream.** Our zero (wrist in the middle) is exactly at Dream's limit. A home taught with the wrist near the middle can land a few degrees on the wrong side without anyone seeing it: on 2026-10-27, left J5 +2.03 and right J5 -4.73.
- What went wrong before the converter: `apply` moved only the **station's home** inside Dream's range (to 0.5 deg inside), not the **end of the taught path**. The driver follows a taught path only when its end is within **2 deg** of the station's home (`READY_HOME_TOLERANCE_DEG`). Left: 2.53 deg apart. Right: 5.73 deg apart. The driver ignored the path and **did not raise the arm** (right arm, 11:28). The preflight did not see it: it compared the bundle's home with the clipped station home and called them equal.

**The converter** (`driver/openarm_mapping.py`, the layer between our angles and Dream's; tests first):
- `logical_limits_deg(side)`: Dream's limits expressed in our angles (the table above).
- `fit_logical(side, pose)`: the same pose with any joint past Dream's range moved 0.5 deg inside, **in our angles**. A pose that fits comes back exactly.
- `home_path_gap_deg(side, station_home, path_end)`: the driver's own test in our degrees. `HOME_MATCH_DEG = 2.0` is now the single number: the driver's `READY_HOME_TOLERANCE_DEG` is set from it.

Where it is used:
1. **Website, Set LEFT/RIGHT home here:** fits the home and the path's last point together right away. The page says "Joint 5 moved -2.5 deg to stay inside Dream's range (the end of the path moved with it)".
2. **Every Save** (`EditorState.to_bundle` → `fit_home_to_dream`): the same fit, so an older bundle saved again is fixed too. `last_fit` records what moved.
3. **Preflight, new check:** "The driver will follow each home path: it ends within 2 deg of the station's home". It caught the left arm on the real files (2.5 deg) before the fix.
4. `apply`: the "moves joint N" note is now silent below 0.01 deg (the bundle's rounding of an already fitted home).
