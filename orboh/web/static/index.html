    ★ Dream-1: tell the robot what to do

    Type any task. Dream-1 looks at the cameras and decides how to reach, grasp and move; the robot only follows action chunks that pass the MuJoCo safety check. Always dry-run first (nothing moves), then approve a real run.

    Simulated arms and rendered cameras: nothing real moves, no approval needed. Dream-1 sees the simulated scene, not your real table.

        5 Simulation safety check
